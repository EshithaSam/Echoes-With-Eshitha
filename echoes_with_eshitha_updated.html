A little about this space

Echoes with Eshitha

A counselling practice built around curiosity, compassion, and collaboration. You don't have to have everything figured out before reaching out.

Compassion, not judgement

Your experiences are met with care and respect, without pressure to be any particular way.

Your pace, your priorities

We work collaboratively around what matters to you, with space to reflect and make sense of things.

A supportive, confidential space

Your privacy and dignity matter. The limits of confidentiality are discussed transparently before we begin.

Ways we can work together

Support for you, your relationships & your workplace

Choose the kind of support that fits your needs. If you're unsure, we can start with a conversation about what you're looking for.

Individual counselling

A space to explore emotions, stress, life changes, self-esteem, boundaries, grief, and recurring patterns with care and curiosity.

One-to-one support

Couple counselling

Employee counselling

Referral-based counselling for work and personal concerns, with a respectful focus on wellbeing, coping, and support.

Workplace support

Psychoeducation workshops

Practical, accessible sessions on stress, emotional wellbeing, self-awareness, coping skills, and mental health literacy.

Manager & HR sensitisation

Sessions to build awareness of mental health at work, supportive conversations, psychological safety, and appropriate referrals.

What to expect

A gentle place to begin

Therapy is a collaborative process, not a test you have to pass.

1

Reach out

Share a little about what support you're looking for and ask any questions you have.

2

Understand your needs

We'll discuss your concerns, expectations, and whether the service feels like a good fit.

3

Work at your pace

We identify meaningful goals together and review what is or isn't helping as we go.

For organisations

Mental wellbeing at work

Preventive, practical mental health support can help employees feel supported and help organisations respond to concerns earlier. Programmes can be tailored to your team's context and priorities.
